? Common Questions

What information should I send for a quotation?

Please share the cap drawing or sample, material, cavity count, machine interface and target output.

Can you make a custom cap mould?

Yes. We review the part geometry, sealing surfaces, cooling, ejection and trial requirements before confirming the design.

What is the normal lead time?

A standard sample mould often takes 15–25 days; production tooling is normally 35–55 days depending on complexity.

Do you test samples before shipment?

Yes. Trial parts, dimensional checks and approved revision notes are kept with the handover package.

Which steels are available?

S136, NAK80, H13 and equivalent grades can be selected according to resin, cycle and service-life targets.

Do you support international buyers?

Yes. We coordinate export packing, documentation and delivery for international projects.

Can you supply auxiliary machines?

Yes. Lining, assembly, folding, slitting and other auxiliary equipment can be coordinated for a complete cell.

How do I start a project?

Send the drawing, sample, resin, cavities, machine information and destination market by email or WhatsApp.
