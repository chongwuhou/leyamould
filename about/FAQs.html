Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our plastic cap molds, auxiliary machines, ordering process, and more. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us directly.

Products & Capabilities

We specialize in water and beverage cap molds, hot fill caps, edible oil caps, soy sauce and condiment closures, medical and cosmetic caps, flip-top, tamper-evident, sports, two-color, wide-mouth, motor oil, 5-gallon, pull-ring and fully custom closure molds.

Yes. We can coordinate cap lining, folding, slitting, pad printing, wadding and combined folding and slitting machines to complete an integrated production cell.

Absolutely. Provide your 2D/3D drawings or a sample cap and our engineering team will review the geometry, material, cavity plan, machine interface and trial requirements before confirming the design.

Ordering & Delivery

To prepare an accurate quotation, we typically need the cap type, 2D/3D design drawings, material, cavity number, injection machine specifications, target production volume and any special requirements such as tamper-evident features or hot runner preferences.

Lead times depend on mold complexity and cavity count. A standard sample mold often requires 15–25 days, while multi-cavity production molds typically require 35–55 days. A detailed timeline is confirmed with your quotation.

Yes. Every project includes trial planning, dimensional checks and sample review before shipment. Approved trial results and revision notes remain connected to the tooling handover.

Payment terms are confirmed in the project quotation according to scope, schedule and commercial requirements. Our team can discuss a practical structure for each order.

Technical & Support

We select quality mold steels such as S136, NAK80, H13 and equivalent grades according to the resin, cycle target, cavity plan and expected service life.

Warranty scope and support terms are defined in the project quotation. We also keep technical records available to make maintenance and future support practical.

Yes. We coordinate export packing and international shipping according to the destination and schedule, with the approved project documentation prepared for handover.

Contact us by email or WhatsApp with the drawing, sample, material, cavity plan, machine information and destination market. We will reply with the next technical questions.
