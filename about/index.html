About XUYI: Plastic Drinkware Supply in Taizhou

台州市旭一家居用品有限公司 (XUYI Bottles) focuses on reusable plastic sports bottles, kids water bottles, large-capacity jugs and shaker bottles for wholesale and brand-customization projects. We operate our own facility and can coordinate selected group/partner resources as required by the product.

THE COMPANY

Focused Product Range. Organized Project Delivery.

Our business connects suitable plastic bottle models with buyer specifications. A retailer may need shelf-ready packaging, a distributor may need reliable model revisions, and a brand may need new artwork, color references or a custom closure.

XUYI is based in Taizhou, Zhejiang, China. The organization has manufacturing and sourcing capabilities and can coordinate selected production routes, with each model’s supply route checked during sourcing.

Our product focus is plastic reusable water bottles, jugs and shaker cups.
