About XUYI: Plastic Drinkware Supply in Taizhou

台州市旭一家居用品有限公司 (XUYI Drinkware) focuses on reusable plastic sports bottles, kids water bottles, large-capacity jugs and shaker bottles for wholesale and brand-customization projects. We operate our own facility and can coordinate selected group/partner resources as required by the product.

THE COMPANY

Focused Product Range. Organized Project Delivery.

Our business connects the right plastic bottle model with the buyer’s specifications. A retailer may need shelf-ready packaging, a distributor may need reliable model revisions, and a brand may need new artwork, color references or a custom closure.

XUYI is based in Taizhou, Zhejiang, China. The organization has factory-backed manufacturing capability and can coordinate selected group and partner production routes, with each model’s supply route checked during sourcing. We do not treat every group photo, equipment item or certification as a separate XUYI facility asset without project-specific verification.

Our public offer covers plastic drinkware in this project. Stainless-steel and glass catalog lines from other group websites are not silently mixed into XUYI’s plastic product catalog.
