Selection, lid formats, packaging and material documentation to ask about.

Bottle use and age suitability

Children’s designs often use straw lids, flip caps and carry straps. Do not assume every kids’ cup is suitable for all ages.

Ask about every component

Material declarations should cover drinking contact surfaces, straw and lid as applicable. Market requirements for children's articles can be stricter.

Verify cleaning and sealing

Ask suppliers for instructions and sample tests that match the exact bottle design and intended use.

Prepare compliant packaging

Country-specific labeling, warnings and reports need review before launch.

Need a specific product recommendation?

Send the models and expected quantity. Our team can confirm actual material, capacity, available customization and relevant project details.
