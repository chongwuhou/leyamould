WHAT THIS SERVICE COVERS

From Idea to an Approved Specification

Production quality is determined by the approved bottle specification, reliable component sourcing and checks at appropriate stages. XUYI coordinates its own and relevant manufacturing-partner processes depending on the model. We do not claim that every part or technique is produced on one particular machine or that a fixed capacity applies to all designs.

01

Translate the approved sample into a production specification

A purchase order should be traceable to a model/variant, component bill of materials, signed sample, artwork revision and pack instruction. For bottle bodies and closures, the specification may include wall dimensions, neck/thread fit, material grade and color. Without a controlled reference, two batches can look similar but have different sealing or label behavior.

Buyer checkpoints
  • Record body, lid, seal and accessory versions
  • Approve a reference sample and printable file
  • Document parts that require buyer approval before substitution
02

Understand forming, assembly and surface-decoration steps

Caps, handles and selected small parts may be injection molded; bottle bodies can use other forming processes based on shape and polymer. Components are then assembled with gaskets, straws or inserts as specified. Printing, transfer or other surface work is checked on the actual substrate. Each process needs suitable controls rather than a universal 'fully automated' promise.

Buyer checkpoints
  • Match tooling method to exact component
  • Check molding flash, threads and clarity
  • Inspect printed and assembled details separately
03

Material identification and incoming quality

Confirm resin grade and component material declarations for body, lid, seals, straw and any colorants. Buyers needing BPA-free or food-contact documentation should state the destination rules and intended use at the start. A report for another brand or product model cannot automatically be treated as this specific bottle's evidence.

Buyer checkpoints
  • Review component-level material list
  • Ask for evidence for destination markets
  • Control resin and color changes
04

In-process and final inspection

Depending on the product, checks may cover visual marks, capacity, dimensions, print position, lid operation, missing seals, assembly cleanliness and selected leak or impact tests. A written sample plan should define the method and acceptance criteria. '100% leakproof' or 'zero defects' should not be used without clear support.

Buyer checkpoints
  • Define critical, major and minor defects
  • Check assembly and close/open consistency
  • Document failures and corrective steps
05

Bulk packing, traceability and shipping release

Review the retail pack and master carton against the approved specification. Check carton counts, size and weight, label information, product color assortments and export handover requirements. The factory and any partner suppliers should know how to identify affected lots when a component or artwork change occurs.

Buyer checkpoints
  • Inspect cartons and labels
  • Retain shipment and batch references
  • Confirm delivery terms and loading instructions
WORKFLOW

Five Steps from Brief to Production

Each stage has an approval point, so production does not rely on assumptions.

  1. 01

    Pre-production review

    Approve model, material, drawings, artwork, pack and incoming requirements.

  2. 02

    Material and production planning

    Confirm feasible forming/assembly methods, supplies, target quantity and schedule.

  3. 03

    Sample and trial checks

    Evaluate dimensions, color, lid operation, decoration and required evidence.

  4. 04

    Production inspections

    Apply agreed in-process and final checks with traceable deviations.

  5. 05

    Packaging and handover

    Verify master cartons, quantity, marks and agreed shipping documents.

BUYER QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Are all XUYI bottles produced in the same factory?

Manufacturing location and processes can differ by model. Confirm the specific production route and any approved partner involvement during quotation.

What inspections are included?

The inspection plan should match the selected SKU and buyer requirements; share any retailer-specific testing or reporting standards.

Can you guarantee zero defects?

No responsible bulk specification should promise absolute zero defects. Agree sampling, critical defects and remediation procedures.

Can I request test reports?

Specify the required destination-market standard and exact components so available declarations or relevant testing can be evaluated.
