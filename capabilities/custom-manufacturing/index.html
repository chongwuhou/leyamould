FAQ

Questions About Custom Orders

Common questions from international importers.

Can you customize bottle colors and logos?

We can review logo artwork, colors and packaging for each selected model. Available decoration methods and order requirements are confirmed during quotation.

What is your MOQ for wholesale orders?

Minimum order quantity depends on bottle model, stock availability and customization. Send us the products and estimated volume to get an accurate answer.

Do you offer samples before bulk production?

Sample availability, lead time and cost are confirmed according to the product and destination. Please include your model number and required custom work.

Which bottle materials can you supply?

PC, Tritan, PP and PETG options are available according to customer requirements. Ask about BPA-free options for a selected model and request documentation for your destination market.

Do you have your own factory?

XUYI operates its own manufacturing facility and may coordinate production through selected partner facilities for some products. We confirm project requirements and applicable quality controls.

How long does production and shipping take?

Production and shipping timelines depend on model, order quantity, materials, custom artwork and destination. We provide an order-specific timeline after reviewing requirements.
