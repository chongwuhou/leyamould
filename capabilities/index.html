DECISION FRAMEWORK

OEM Branding vs. New Product Development

A clear distinction between the two scopes prevents unexpected tooling costs and sample changes.

Existing-model OEM

  • Choose a current plastic model, body and lid configuration
  • Discuss logo, color, graphics, box and quantity
  • Confirm feasibility on a decorated physical sample
  • Agree approved version and carton specification

New-design ODM

  • Provide drawings, reference dimensions and use case
  • Review body and component engineering constraints
  • Assess tooling, prototypes and intellectual-property terms
  • Test and approve the production-representative sample
BUYER HANDOFF

What to Include in an OEM / ODM RFQ

Product and design

Model or sketches, volume, material and lid requirements, dimensions and target users.

Artwork and variants

Vector logo, color references, carton layout, quantities per variation and sample expectations.

Compliance and delivery

Destination market, documents, test scope, packing list, preferred shipping term and timeline.
