THE PRACTICAL ANSWER

What Is the Difference Between OEM and ODM Plastic Bottle Manufacturing?

The project route affects tooling, development cost, approval time and what information the buyer needs to provide.

In a model-based OEM or private-label project, the buyer chooses an existing XUYI bottle model and asks about decorating or packing it for a brand. This may involve a logo, artwork, an available cap color, a straw option or a specific carton. Each choice is checked against the actual body and closure; a photograph alone cannot establish what is available.

In a deeper ODM project, the buyer requests design changes such as a new bottle profile, adjusted grip, new lid mechanism or modified capacity. This may need drawings, engineering review, tooling and physical prototypes. A request for a 'new design' is not the same as a guaranteed new mold: feasibility, tool ownership, intellectual property and cost must be agreed in writing.

XUYI operates its own manufacturing facility in Taizhou and may coordinate selected partner-made styles. Buyers should confirm which facility and process applies to the selected model. We focus exclusively on plastic drinkware in this catalog, not glass bottles or stainless-steel insulated tumblers.

BEFORE REQUESTING A QUOTE

What to Send for an Accurate Custom Bottle Quote

A brief containing these details lets the sourcing team ask fewer follow-up questions.

  • Catalog model code or photos with preferred body, lid and capacity.
  • Order volume by model, color and version; clarify whether future repeat orders are expected.
  • Vector logo artwork, available Pantone/color references and printing locations.
  • Body, cap, straw and seal material requirements; specify any needed testing or regulatory documents.
  • Country of sale and use case: gym, school, promotional or general hydration.
  • Packaging style, barcode / label requirements and carton marking instructions.
  • Sample approval needs, expected dispatch window and shipping destination.
  • For ODM: drawings, target dimensions, functionality, sample reference and tooling/IP questions.
XUYI BUYER NOTE

Choose an Existing Model First When Possible

Many private-label projects need only model confirmation and decoration feasibility, not a new bottle mold. Starting from a current catalog model can make requirements easier to define, but it does not establish a specific price, minimum order or lead time.

Ask how the chosen body, lid and decoration will be checked on a physical sample. For genuinely new structures, agree drawing revisions, mold ownership and approval responsibilities before any tooling commitment.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

OEM / ODM Plastic Water Bottle FAQs

Direct answers to questions brands and wholesale importers ask before requesting a quotation.

Can I put my own logo on XUYI plastic water bottles?

Send the model code, vector logo, preferred placement, colors and quantity. We can review model-specific printing or labeling options and confirm feasibility in a quote.

What is the difference between OEM and ODM water bottles?

OEM commonly starts with an existing design and applies agreed branding or options. ODM may involve new product design, drawings, prototypes and tooling. The actual project scope must be agreed.

Is a new mold required for a customized water bottle?

Not always. Existing-model branding may avoid new structural tooling. A changed shape, capacity or closure can require mold evaluation and additional prototypes.

What is the minimum order quantity for private-label bottles?

There is no single verified MOQ across all models. It changes with stock status, printing method, color, packing and tooling, so provide your model and volume for confirmation.

Can you make BPA-free or food-contact-compliant bottles?

Do not infer compliance from appearance or a polymer name. Specify the exact model, body and lid materials, intended food-contact use and destination regulations; matching documentation must be reviewed before claims are made.

Can I review a sample before bulk production?

Sample availability, decoration proofs, fees, schedule and shipping are confirmed for the selected model. Bulk orders should be checked against an approved reference sample.

Do you offer design exclusivity or NDAs?

Discuss confidentiality and design/tooling ownership before sharing sensitive drawings. Any NDA, exclusivity or tooling rights require a specific agreement rather than an assumed website promise.
