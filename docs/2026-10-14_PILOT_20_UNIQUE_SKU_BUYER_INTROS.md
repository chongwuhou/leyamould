# P1 — first 20 SKU buyer introductions in five locales (2026-10-14)

The XUYI owner requested true model-level differentiation, not more copy generated by changing capacity and title in the same product detail paragraph. Select only 5 verified models from each of four collections as a **pilot**; don't automatically replace every product body.

| Category | Five SKU slugs | Verified source distinctions |
|---|---|---|
| Sports water bottles | 2501, 2502, 2505, 2507, 2095 | 550, 750, 800, 1200, 500 ml; PC/PP/ABS for the first four, PC/PP for 2095 |
| Kids water bottles | 2508, 2509, 2106, 2108, 2408 | 600, 550, 750, 650, 1200 ml; 2106 source lists PC/PP rather than PC/PP/ABS |
| Large capacity water jugs | 2290, 2281, 2292, 2215, 2271 | 1600, 2200, 3800, 2000, 2300 ml; PC/PP/ABS catalog components |
| Plastic protein shaker bottles | 501, 502, 507, 509, 510 | 500, 700, 500, 400, 400 ml; source-reviewed PP/PE or PP and original measurements |

**Editing sources**
- `data/pilot-20-sku-openings-en.json`: 20 individual 117–129-word English editorials.
- `data/pilot-20-sku-openings-{sports,kids,jugs,shakers}-locales.json`: 80 independent native-language editorials.
- `data/pilot-20-sku-openings-locale-addenda.json`: 60 model-specific finishing passages to keep the translated introductions in a comparable 100–150-word band.
- `data/pilot-20-sku-d1-body-baselines.json`: immutable hashes for the currently approved 80 D1 locale product bodies.
- `sku_unique_openings.py::editor_intro`: reusable source rendering component. It **replaces** the old generic paragraph immediately following the product H1, before specifications. Does not rewrite `dist/`, add a duplicate module, disturb Product JSON-LD or alter SEO headings, facts, inquiry buttons or media.
- `site_renderer.py`: English uses this component only when the entire English product body is still source/template-owned; the existing `BODY_OVERRIDES` guard prevents overwriting an editor-authored body.
- `scripts/render_locales.py`: the translator uses the editorials at the D1-to-source render stage, only if the entire original D1 `mainHtml` field matches its frozen owner baseline. **Any** later owner edit, even outside the opening, causes the entire pilot replacement to be skipped. Other SKU bodies are entirely unchanged.

**Accuracy limits:** source catalog lacks verified cap types for most SKUs. The editorial paragraphs describe *which lid and seal questions to ask* rather than asserting straw/flip/screw mechanisms or leak-proof performance. Buyers, applications and size comparisons are framed as candidate sourcing scenarios, not model certifications. No invented safety, compliance, BPA-free, production, accessories, pricing or materials.

**Guard tests:** `scripts/test_pilot_20_unique_sku_openings.py` checks 4×5 model split, 100 five-language product pages with unique 100–150-word paragraphs, zero duplicates, source-data consistency, CMS owner-edited full-body overrides, English owner guard, double-build idempotency and unknown new SKU no-op. The CI release gate runs before Cloudflare Pages deployment. All unrelated product URLs, original R2 resources, brand logos and inquiry forms are unchanged.

**Interpretation:** extra differentiated source content improves utility and distinctness for these pilot models, but does not by itself guarantee a particular SEO similarity score or Google ranking. Other product introductions remain untouched pending genuine SKU evidence and editorial prioritization.
