## Publish criteria
- Use useful, good-quality, visibly unwatermarked images. Serve existing source assets from XUYI R2, not by hotlinking to sibling sites.
- Match context: plastic drinkware photos for XUYI products; factory and warehouse photos for processes; quality apparatus images for general QA discussion.
- Do not represent stainless steel, glass, different materials, or unrelated machines as the exact manufacturing route for a specific XUYI SKU.
- Avoid unverified SKU-specific certifications, output numbers, test results and equipment ownership claims.
- Customer-brand logos are separate from permission to reuse group-owned photography. Do not imply customer endorsements without verifying the relationship.
- Remove public third-party-source disclaimers and competitor-site links; provenance may be retained internally.
- Approved image sources remain in the private group media inventory and R2 allowlist.
