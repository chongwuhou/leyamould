Engine Oil Cap Mould FAQ

Which steel works for engine oil?

Corrosion-resistant mould steel is selected against the fluid, resin and maintenance plan.

How is leakage controlled?

The thread, plug and sealing lip are reviewed together and checked during trial.

Can you make 5L and 1L versions?

Yes. The bottle finish, torque target and cavity plan are reviewed for each package.

What is the service life?

The reference tooling direction is designed for millions of cycles with planned maintenance.
