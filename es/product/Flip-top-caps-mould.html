Preguntas frecuentes

Molde de tapón cosmético flip-top FAQ

How many cycles can the hinge run?

The reference design is tested beyond 50,000 open-close cycles; the final target is confirmed with the buyer.

Can the cap use a custom orifice?

Yes. Orifice diameter, flow and decorative finish can be reviewed together.

Can you match an existing cosmetic cap?

Send the cap, bottle finish or drawing for a hinge and seal assessment.

Do you support dual-colour designs?

Two-shot and insert strategies can be evaluated during DFM.
