Molde de tapón para envases alimentarios FAQ

Can the mould run hot-fill packaging?

Yes, when the cap, resin and process temperatures are included in the technical brief.

How do you protect food-contact surfaces?

Steel traceability, polishing and cleaning expectations are recorded before machining.

Can you match a sauce cap?

Send a sample or drawing and we will review the dispensing and sealing geometry.

Do you support tamper evidence?

Snap-on, screw-on and tamper-band options can be reviewed during DFM.
