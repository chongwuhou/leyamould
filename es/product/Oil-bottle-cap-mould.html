Preguntas frecuentes

Molde de tapón para aceite comestible FAQ

What steel is best for edible oil moulds?

ASSAB S136 or NAK80 with vacuum heat treatment provides corrosion resistance and cleanable surfaces.

How do you guarantee leak-proof caps?

The sealing geometry is reviewed and every mould is trialled with actual oil filling.

Can you match an existing oil cap?

Send a drawing or physical sample for a DFM review and design match.

What is the typical mould life?

With planned maintenance, edible-oil moulds are designed to exceed 5 million cycles.
