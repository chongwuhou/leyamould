FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

We’re Here to Answer
All Your Questions.

Which plastics are used in your bottles?

Materials vary by product component. We verify the actual SKU and relevant destination-market requirements before making material or food-contact claims.

Do you offer samples before production?

Please give the model, destination and customization needs. We can then discuss sample availability, cost and shipping.

Can you make a custom bottle design?

We can review artwork, existing designs and the feasibility of new tooling or component changes. Each proposal needs technical confirmation.

Can I request custom colors or packaging?

Describe your brand colors, logo, labels and packaging. Feasibility depends on the selected model and order.

How do you handle factory quality checks?

XUYI has its own facility and may coordinate selected partners. We agree project-specific samples, specifications and checkpoints.
