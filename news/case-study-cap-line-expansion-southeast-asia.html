01The direct answer

Start with drawing revision, neck finish, resin family, cavity target, press interface, trial parts and acceptance points. Buyer checklist

  1. approved drawing revision and critical dimensions
  2. neck finish, thread or sealing datum
  3. resin family, supplier data and batch variation
  4. target cavity count and cavity identification
  5. machine interface and available process signals
  6. cooling, venting and ejection review
  7. representative sample and conditioning method
  8. dimensional, visual, torque or leak acceptance
  9. spare-part and maintenance boundary
  10. change-control owner and trial sign-off

Frequently asked questions

What is the first file to send for beverage line tooling expansion brief?

Send the latest approved drawing or a representative sample together with resin, machine and acceptance information. If one value is open, label it as open instead of guessing it.

Does this article promise a fixed mould result?

No. It is a buyer-side review framework. The final geometry, process window and evidence must be agreed for the actual part and machine.

Can the same mould brief be reused for another closure?

Only after the neck finish, material, sealing function and quality limits are rechecked. Similar-looking caps can have different tooling risks.

What should be agreed before handover?

Agree the drawing revision, sample record, inspection method, open items, spare-part boundary and sign-off owner in writing.
