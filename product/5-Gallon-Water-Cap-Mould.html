5 Gallon Water Cap Mould FAQ

Can the mould support reusable water bottles?

Yes. The bottle finish, plug seal and sanitisation conditions are included in the tooling brief.

How is the tear band checked?

The bridge layout and opening force are agreed and verified during trial.

Can the cap fit a different dispenser?

Send the dispenser and bottle interface for a compatibility review.

Do you provide trial samples?

Agreed samples, fit checks and inspection evidence are included before handover.
