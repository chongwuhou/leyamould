Technology

Inside Our Edible Oil Cap Mould Engineering

Leak-Proof Seal & Conformal Cooling

Moldflow-reviewed sealing rings and conformal cooling keep every cavity round and consistent.

  • ✓ Optimized gate placement
  • ✓ 3D-printed cooling inserts
  • ✓ Thermal imaging verification

Food-Grade Steel & Anti-Corrosion Treatment

Mirror-polished stainless mould steel reduces bacterial adhesion and makes cleaning easier.

  • ✓ ASSAB S136 or NAK80
  • ✓ Vacuum heat treatment
  • ✓ Full material traceability

Multi-Cavity Consistency & Quick Change

Balanced inserts let the buyer expand output or replace a single cavity with less downtime.

  • ✓ 24 to 72 cavity options
  • ✓ Individual nozzle control
  • ✓ Quick-change inserts
