Common Questions

PET Preform Mould FAQ

Which cavity counts are possible?

The cavity plan is matched to the bottle, machine and output target, from pilot configurations to high-cavity production.

Can you make edible-oil preforms?

Yes. The resin, neck finish, wall distribution and stretch-blow target are reviewed together.

How do you control preform weight?

Runner balance, gate design, cooling and cavity-by-cavity weight checks are used.

Can a preform mould be upgraded?

A modular insert and cavity strategy can be evaluated when the machine and geometry allow it.
