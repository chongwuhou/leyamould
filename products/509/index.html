Frequently Asked Questions

Can I order custom printed model 509?

Logo placement, decoration process and minimum order quantity are confirmed against the actual product, artwork and order quantity.

What capacity and plastic material does this SKU use?

The catalog reference lists 400ml capacity and PP+PE materials. Request component-level verification and market-specific food-contact documents before confirming your order.

Is this bottle BPA-free and leakproof?

BPA-free material options or leak testing can be requested. Neither claim is guaranteed for every variant; confirm test evidence, cap design and intended usage conditions.

How do I request a sample and shipping quote?

Send the model, target quantity, destination, logo details and pack type. The team will confirm availability, sample costs and appropriate trade terms.
