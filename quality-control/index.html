Quality Control & Documentation

Quality processes and supporting material documents must match the specific model and destination market.

Quality Focus

Product Quality Depends on the Details

We confirm project-specific information before making material or certification claims.

Material Identification

Verify the actual components and plastic material of the selected bottle. A multi-material entry is not evidence of BPA-free status.

Sample Confirmation

Review lid function, capacity, colors, printing and packing against the agreed sample.

Production & Packing Checks

Agree on measurable inspection points, carton configuration and product condition prior to shipment.

Testing Scope

Request reports for the actual SKU, materials and applicable destination-market requirements.

Supplier Coordination

For relevant partner-made styles, confirm how requirements and acceptance checks are handled.

Traceable Order Details

Maintain clear records of model number, variant, artwork and final approved specification.

We do not publish an unverified FDA, LFGB, BPA-free, Tritan, food-safe or other compliance claim. Ask us about specific documentation for your selected models.
Buyer Questions

Quality FAQs

Key compliance questions to include in your sourcing brief.

What plastic materials are used?

Our source catalog lists PP, PE, PC and other component materials on different models. Material specifications and food-contact documentation need checking for each model and destination market.

Do you have your own factory?

XUYI operates its own manufacturing facility and may coordinate production through selected partner facilities for some products. We confirm project requirements and applicable quality controls.

How long does production and shipping take?

Production and shipping timelines depend on model, order quantity, materials, custom artwork and destination. We provide an order-specific timeline after reviewing requirements.

Can I send a product drawing or a similar product sample?

Yes, you can describe the target design and send artwork or specifications. Our team can evaluate what can be supplied or customized and whether tooling is needed.

Can we mix colors or different models in one order?

Possible combinations depend on the bottle series, packing process and order quantity. Include your requested color and model breakdown when asking for a quote.

Can you provide test reports for my export market?

Please specify the model, destination country and required standards. Any documents offered must match the actual product, material and valid test scope.
