THE PRACTICAL ANSWER

How to Prepare an Export-Ready Plastic Bottle Order

Large bottles often occupy more freight volume than their weight suggests, so a quote should distinguish unit price from packed-shipment cost.

A wholesale purchase order needs more than bottle photographs. Ask for the exact SKU and color breakdown, individual package type, pieces per master carton, outer carton length, width and height, gross weight, net weight and total carton count. Large water jugs and bottles with handles may require different carton density or protective packing than compact sports bottles.

Freight options are not interchangeable. Courier/express may be considered for samples, air freight for smaller urgent orders, and sea freight for larger planned shipments. The commercially suitable option depends on packed volume, chargeable weight, destination, customs obligations and your delivery deadline.

Terms such as EXW, FOB, FCA, CIF, DAP or DDP allocate responsibilities differently. Do not assume a supplier quote includes duties, destination taxes, customs clearance or final-mile delivery unless that scope is written into the order and appropriate for the shipment. Shipment tracking and document availability are subject to the selected carrier and agreed service.

BEFORE REQUESTING A QUOTE

What International Buyers Should Include in a Shipping RFQ

Accurate carton and delivery data can prevent a low unit price from producing an unexpectedly high landed cost.

  • Product codes, capacity variants, color breakdowns and carton quantities.
  • Packing type: bulk wrapping, individual retail box, custom gift pack or protective divider.
  • Pieces per inner pack and master carton; gross/net weight and carton dimensions.
  • Required product labels, barcode formats, country-of-origin text and carton marks.
  • Delivery country, city, port or postal code and whether a forwarder is already appointed.
  • Desired shipping mode, cargo-ready date and any hard receiving window.
  • Proposed Incoterm with named place and agreed responsibility for export/import clearance.
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, relevant material documents and any customer-specific warehouse requirements.
XUYI BUYER NOTE

Clarify 'Delivered Price' Before Comparing Quotes

EXW, FOB and DDP are not interchangeable quote labels. Each has different cost and risk responsibilities; the named place and applicable Incoterms edition should be written into the contract. For retail shipments, labeling and warehouse handling fees can materially affect the total.

A large-capacity plastic jug may have low unit weight but require more carton space. Ask for actual packed-volume data and compare freight based on cartons, not only nominal bottle volume.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Plastic Water Bottle Packing & Shipping FAQs

What procurement teams need to know about cartons, carriers, documentation and landed cost.

Can XUYI ship plastic water bottles internationally?

International shipping arrangements can be reviewed for the destination and selected order. The route, carrier, responsible party, charges and any restrictions must be confirmed before booking.

What shipping documents should I expect?

Typically a commercial invoice and packing list are discussed, with transport documents and other declarations determined by the carrier, Incoterms, destination and product. Confirm the exact list per order.

Do you support FOB, EXW, CIF or DDP shipping terms?

Trade terms are discussed case by case; listing a term is not a promise it is offered. Confirm the Incoterm, named place, duties, clearance and delivery scope in the quotation.

How do I calculate freight for a bulky plastic water jug?

You need packed carton measurements, gross weight, total cartons, and the freight mode. Carriers may use chargeable or volumetric weight, so the bottle's empty weight alone is insufficient.

Can bottles be packed in custom retail boxes?

Packaging options depend on the actual SKU, required artwork, quantity and sourcing feasibility. Send dielines, barcodes and shelf/warehouse requirements for review.

Can I combine kids bottles, sports bottles and shakers in one shipment?

A combined order may be discussed, but model/color quantities, production readiness, customs classification, packing and shipping costs need separate confirmation.

How long does shipping take to the US or EU?

There is no universal verified shipping duration. Timing varies by destination, mode, season, carrier schedules and customs processing. Request a route-specific estimate after carton details are confirmed.
